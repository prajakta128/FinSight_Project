# FinSight

### From Transactions to Smarter Business Decisions

FinSight is an AI-powered financial intelligence platform designed for **Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs)**.

It goes beyond basic expense tracking by helping businesses **track financial activity, understand spending, detect risks, forecast cash flow, simulate decisions, and take action** from a single platform.

---

## Problem

Many MSMEs manage their finances using spreadsheets, notebooks, invoices, and disconnected tools.

This makes it difficult to:

- Track expenses and revenue in one place
- Monitor receivables and payables
- Identify unusual or increasing expenses
- Understand cash-flow trends
- Predict upcoming financial pressure
- Analyze vendor and customer behavior
- Make data-driven financial decisions quickly

FinSight addresses these challenges through a unified financial intelligence platform.
